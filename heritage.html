Basotho Blanket

The Basotho blanket is more than just clothing: it is a symbol of identity, pride and unity. Its unique designs and colours tell stories of status, age and community.

The History of Thaba Bosiu

Thaba Bosiu is one of the most important historical places in Lesotho. In the early 19th century, King Moshoeshoe I established his stronghold on this flat-topped mountain. Its steep sides provided natural protection during times of conflict, helping him protect his people and build the Basotho nation.

The mountain became a symbol of Basotho unity, courage and resistance. Today, Thaba Bosiu remains an important heritage site where visitors can learn about King Moshoeshoe I and the history of the Basotho people.
